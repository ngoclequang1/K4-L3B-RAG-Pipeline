# Student Clubs & Associations

**Source:** https://vinuni.edu.vn/student_life/student-clubs-associations/

**Crawled:** 2026-09-25T09:35:00+07:00

---

VinUniversity provides a broad range of student-led clubs and societies. The public directory groups activities into areas such as sport, academic interests, community service, arts and culture, and student associations. The page presents clubs as opportunities for students to develop knowledge, practical skills, leadership, teamwork, communication, and peer connections.

## Examples of clubs
### Sport
- VinUni Golf Club (VUGC)
- VinUni Ultimate Club (VUC)
- VinUni Swimming Club (DiveVin)
- VinUni Kendo Club
- VinUni Volleyball Club (SPIKE)
- VinUni Tennis Club
- VinUni Gaming Society (VGS)
- VinUni Football Team
- VinUni Badminton Club
- VinUni Basketball Team (VUBT)

### Academic and professional interests
The directory includes VinUniversity Debating Club, VinTelligence for Data Science and AI, Youth Hospitality Association, Vinnovation, VinUni Research Club, Vin Pioneers, GDSC VinUni, VinUni Finance Society, VinUni Business Insider, and VinUni Algorithm Club.

### Arts and community
Other listed groups include Ecoquity, Vinista, VinViVid, VinUni Harmony, Vin-A-House Dance Crew, and VinUni Classical Music Club. The directory also lists the Student Council and the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union branch.

## Use for student help
Students can use the directory to discover organizations matching their interests and identify the relevant club or association before asking about membership or activities.
