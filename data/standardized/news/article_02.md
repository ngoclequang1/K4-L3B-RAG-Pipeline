# [ANNOUNCEMENT] Launch of the New Student Portal for Summer 2026 Course Registration

**Source:** https://registrar.vinuni.edu.vn/2026/06/29/announcement-launch-of-the-new-student-portal-for-summer-2026-course-registration/
**Crawled:** 2026-09-25T09:35:00+07:00

---

Published June 29, 2026 by the Office of the University Registrar. Starting with the Summer 2026 semester, VinUni course registration is conducted through the new VinUniDigi Student Portal. The portal is designed to let students search, register, review, and manage courses in one place.

## Handbook topics
The linked Course Registration Handbook covers signing in, registering for courses, reading class status, handling common registration issues, reviewing registered classes, dropping classes, previewing the timetable, and completing the final registration checklist.

## Important rules
- Available classes can be registered.
- Full classes have no remaining seats.
- Conflict indicates an overlap with another registered class.
- Prerequisites and pre-study requirements are checked automatically.
- A registered class can be dropped only while the registration period is open.

## Final checklist
Before confirming registration, students should verify the academic term, prerequisites, class availability, timetable conflicts, and registration status, then preview the timetable.
