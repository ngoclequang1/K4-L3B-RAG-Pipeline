# VinUni Research Day & Bootcamp 2026

Published June 5, 2026, this news article introduces VinUniversity's Research Day & Bootcamp 2026, a university-wide research showcase focused on interdisciplinary collaboration and student-led inquiry. The event was scheduled for June 6, 2026, from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM at the VinUniversity campus.

## Organization and goals
The event was organized by the Research Management Office and the Student Research Club, together with the university's Colleges and RISE Centers. Its purpose was to demonstrate research outcomes, connect researchers across disciplines, and create opportunities for students to explore research and potential collaborations.

## Program pillars
- **Macro-Perspectives & Trends:** a keynote on developments in science, technology, innovation, and Vietnam's development journey.
- **Interactive Exhibition & Networking:** research clusters around People, Planet, and Prosperity, with laboratory booths, social experiments, posters, and networking.
- **Hands-On Bootcamp & Project Funding:** research case studies, interdisciplinary problem-solving workshops, and a funding track where student teams could present ongoing work to potential supporters.

## Relevance to students
The article highlights the event as a place to identify research opportunities, meet potential collaborators, understand current research activity at VinUni, and explore support for student projects.
