# Current Students - Scholarship and Financial Aid

**Source:** https://admissions.vinuni.edu.vn/scholarship-and-financial-aid/current-students/
**Crawled:** 2026-09-25T09:35:00+07:00

---

VinUniversity publishes a dedicated page describing financial assistance and grant options for current or prospective students. The page includes a range of support programs and explains value, eligibility, application timing, and supporting requirements.

## Bursary
The bursary is described as supplementary assistance for enrolled students facing verified unexpected financial hardship, such as sudden loss of family income or medical emergencies. The published value is 5% to 65% of tuition, with awards covering a semester or academic year. Applications are reviewed twice annually, in July and November. Applicants need to be current full-time students in good academic and disciplinary standing and submit the required financial-aid documentation.

## Future Leader Grant
The page describes a grant worth 10% of tuition for eligible students who already hold a qualifying merit-based scholarship and can demonstrate financial hardship affecting the remaining tuition payment. The grant can be renewable yearly when eligibility and performance conditions are met.

## Student Shine Grant
The Student Shine Grant provides support for approved national or international competitions, conferences, and professional platforms. The published limits differ by competition type and whether support is for an individual or team.

## Student help
The page links students to financial-aid application guidance and related scholarship information, making it a reference point for questions about eligibility, timing, and required documents.
