## Quick guide
Students representing VinUni at external events should register at least one week in advance and
obtain the required College endorsement and SAM approval before using the VinUni name.
Students seeking University grant support should normally register at least 10 working days before
the event.
Students organizing an event under the VinUni name should register at least two weeks before
announcing the event; additional rules apply to organizing activities.
## I-II. Purpose and applicability
The guidelines govern representation and event organization under the VinUniversity name and are
intended to protect students' experience and the University's reputation while requiring
professionalism, integrity, and compliance.
They apply when a student officially represents VinUni, publicly uses a VinUni identifier at an external
event, or organizes/co-organizes an event using a VinUni identifier.
They do not generally apply to ordinary coursework or private participation in external events when
no VinUni identifier, endorsement, or funding is involved.
## III. Representation and approval
Before public use of a VinUni identifier, the student must obtain the required approval or
endorsement. SAM coordinates the review and approval process.
A registration should include event details, the student's role, and how the event supports academic
or professional development.
## IV. Funding and grants
When VinUni funds an event, students must follow applicable financial procedures, including prior
approval, receipts, and post-event reporting.
Grant applications should be made before the event, normally at least 10 working days ahead, and
earlier for international travel or visa matters.
Typical eligible costs can include registration, travel, accommodation, and other approved
participation-related expenses, subject to the current grant regulation.
## V-VI. Event organization and responsibility
Written approval is required before announcing an event or using a VinUni identifier. SAM may
coordinate with Marketing/Brand, Finance, and Legal as applicable.
Students must not sign contracts, MOUs, sponsorship agreements, or other commitments on behalf of
VinUniversity. Contracts and commitments must be routed through the responsible University unit.
Event organizers must assess safety, meet venue/insurance/licensing requirements, and handle
participant personal data in line with applicable data-protection law.
A short post-event report is due to FAO and SAM within 7 calendar days; when funding is received,
financial and outcome documents are due within 14 calendar days.
