## 1. Purpose and scope
The guideline establishes a consistent governance framework for VinUniversity student clubs, teams,
and associations. The framework is intended to support student development and campus life while
requiring safe, transparent, responsible operation and compliance with University requirements.
It applies to student-led clubs, teams, and associations officially established or recognized by
VinUniversity, including academic/professional, sports/arts/cultural, community service/social impact,
and interest/innovation groups.
Recognition does not give a club separate legal status or authorize students to enter agreements or
make commitments on behalf of VinUniversity. Applicable law and University-level policies prevail in
case of inconsistency.
## 2. Principles
Student-led governance operates under guidance and oversight from the Student Affairs Management
Office (SAM).
Inclusiveness and equality require voluntary, equitable participation free from discrimination,
bullying, coercion, harassment, and retaliation.
Transparency and accountability require regular reporting, proper documentation, and proportionate
controls.
Learning integration encourages clubs to connect activities with academic learning, research, and
community engagement.
## 3. Club formation and recognition
A club seeking recognition must have clear objectives aligned with the University mission and one of
the six development pillars, without substantial duplication of an existing club.
A minimum of 10 active members is required. The Executive Committee must include, at minimum, a
President, an Operations/Secretary focal point, and a Finance focal point.
A new club completes a one-semester probationary period. An active club renews recognition once
each academic year using Form CLB-01.
The Head of Student Development makes the recognition decision within a target of 10 working days
after SAM confirms the application is complete.
## 4. Funding, events, safety and data
Recognized clubs can receive approved financial and non-financial support. Expenditures must use
University-approved channels and have complete documentation.
Activities involving budgets, partners/sponsors, external attendees, public communications,
off-campus or overnight activity, permits, sensitive data, or safety risks require Form CLB-02 at least
10 working days in advance.
Complex or high-risk activities may require at least 20 working days advance submission and
additional approval where external authorities are involved.
The club must identify risks, designate a responsible person, establish suitable emergency
arrangements, and immediately report emergencies through the appropriate support channel.
## 5. Corrective action and appeals
Measures can range from a reminder/request for information to a corrective action plan, suspension,
or withdrawal of recognition, depending on the issue and risk.
A club may submit a written appeal within 10 working days of a decision to suspend or withdraw
recognition, stating the grounds and supporting evidence.
